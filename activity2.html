Paragraph

A paragraph is a fundamental unit of writing that presents a single idea or topic, organized into one or more sentences that form a coherent and logical whole. It is typically marked by an indentation on the first line and helps structure text, making it easier for readers to follow the author's points. A well-structured paragraph includes a topic sentence to introduce the main idea and supporting sentences to elaborate with details, examples, and context, often concluding with a sentence that bridges to the next idea
